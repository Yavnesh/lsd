# 04 - Content Pipeline

## The Law of Infinite Expansion

To prevent content rot and ensure the Knowledge Universe can expand infinitely, we follow a strict content pipeline. The AI generation engine must adhere to these rules when creating new Regions and Topics.

### 1. The Structure of a Topic (The Atomic Unit)
Every Topic must contain exactly:
1. **The Hook:** A 1-2 sentence realization that breaks an existing mental model.
2. **The Meme:** A relatable pop-culture or internet format that instantly grounds the complex topic in everyday reality.
3. **The Core Framework:** A 3-4 bullet point summary of the concept.
4. **The Trial (Quiz):** A single, highly contextual multiple-choice question designed to test comprehension, not memorization.
5. **The Mind-Blowing Fact:** An obscure, highly shareable fact related to the topic.
6. **The Share Card:** The final summary to export to social media.

### 2. World and Region Generation Rules
- **Worlds** must represent massive, universal domains of human knowledge (e.g., Psychology, Finance, History, Physics).
- **Regions** are specific sub-domains inside a World. Every World must launch with at least 1 Visible Region and 1 Hidden Region (Fog of War).
- **Topics** are the individual nodes within a Region. A Region must have between 3 and 7 Topics before it is considered a valid cluster.

### 3. The Quality Gate
Before an AI-generated Topic is pushed to production, it must pass the "Bar Test": *If you explained this concept to a friend at a bar using these exact words, would they think you are smart, or would they think you are reciting a textbook?* If it sounds like a textbook, it fails the pipeline.
