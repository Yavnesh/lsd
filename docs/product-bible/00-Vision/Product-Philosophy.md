# Product Philosophy

## The North Star
**LearnSomethingDaily (LSD) is a Knowledge RPG.** 
Users do not "take lessons" or "collect flashcards." They explore a persistent, ever-expanding universe of knowledge. Every action taken builds upon their personal identity within this universe. The primary goal is to foster an emotional attachment to learning through exploration, collection, and identity evolution.

## The Shift
- **Old Paradigm:** Knowledge Inventory (Lists, Categories, Lessons)
- **New Paradigm:** Knowledge Universe (Worlds, Regions, Topography, Discovery)

## The 7 Product Pillars
Every feature must support at least one of these pillars:

1. **Learn:** Acquire practical, high-impact knowledge quickly.
2. **Explore:** Discover new topics organically. The universe must feel vast and uncharted (utilizing "Fog of War" mechanics).
3. **Collect:** Build a personal knowledge universe. Mastery yields tangible, visual artifacts (e.g., Knowledge Crystals).
4. **Master:** Deepen understanding through spaced repetition, advanced trials, and boss battles.
5. **Compete:** Compare progress, engage in PvP trials, and participate in seasonal challenges.
6. **Reflect:** Review growth, visualize the interconnectedness of topics (Knowledge Atlas), and trace the learning journey.
7. **Share:** Spread insights and attract new users through easily shareable "Eureka" moments and Artifact showcases.

## Long-Term Retention Philosophy
We design for years, not days.
- **7 Days:** Establish the core loop. Hook the user with their first Artifact and an Identity promotion (e.g., *Curious Human* → *Explorer*).
- **30 Days:** Unveil the scope of the universe. Introduce seasonal content and regional mastery.
- **90+ Days:** Deepen the meta-game with hidden secrets, legacy badges, and competitive leaderboard prestige.
