# 01 - Features and Personas

## Target Personas

### 1. The Curious Explorer
- **Demographic:** 18-35, highly inquisitive, values personal growth.
- **Goal:** To understand the world and themselves better.
- **Pain Point:** Finds traditional learning boring, dry, or disconnected from real life.
- **RPG Motivator:** The Fog of War. They want to clear the map, collect all the lore, and discover hidden regions. The *explorationScore* mechanic is designed for them.

### 2. The Competitive Scholar
- **Demographic:** 16-28, competitive, enjoys systems and optimization.
- **Goal:** To be the smartest in the room and have proof of it.
- **Pain Point:** Learning apps feel solitary and lack stakes.
- **RPG Motivator:** The Global Rating (Elo) and Identity Titles. They want to climb from "Curious Human" to "Mastermind" and flaunt Epic Artifacts on their profile.
