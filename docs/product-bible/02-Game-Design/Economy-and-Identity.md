# Game Economy & Identity

## The Identity System
We answer the question: *Who does the user become?*
Instead of just displaying an Elo rating, users earn **Titles** that represent their holistic progression through the Knowledge Universe. These are prominently displayed on their profile and in PvP matches.

### Title Progression Path
1. **Curious Human** (Default)
2. **Explorer** (Unlocks after discovering 5 Topics)
3. **Researcher** (Unlocks upon mastering 1 Region)
4. **Scholar** (Unlocks after 30-day streak)
5. **Thinker** (Unlocks upon mastering 3 Regions)
6. **Analyst** (Unlocks after discovering a Hidden Secret)
7. **Scientist** (Unlocks upon mastering an entire World)
8. **Sage** (Unlocks at 100-day streak + 2 Worlds mastered)
9. **Mastermind** (Top 5% PvP Elo)
10. **Grand Archivist** (100% completion of 5 Worlds)
11. **Living Encyclopedia** (The ultimate flex; requires immense, sustained dedication)

## The Collectibles (Artifacts)
Badges are out. **Artifacts** are in.
When a user masters a region or world, they don't get a flat 2D badge. They earn a stylized collectible (e.g., *The Crystal of Cognition*, *The Scroll of Habits*). 
- Artifacts are stored in the **Artifact Vault** (formerly Achievement Cabinet).
- Each artifact has Rarity (Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary).
- Artifacts provide permanent, passive bonuses (e.g., *The Crystal of Cognition* grants +5% XP when exploring the Psychology World).

## Game Economy (Currencies)
- **XP (Experience Points):** Measures raw effort and time investment. Drives the leveling system (Level 1, Level 2, etc.).
- **Elo Rating (Mastery/PvP):** Measures accuracy and speed relative to others. Fluctuates up and down based on performance.
- **Knowledge Crystals (Premium/Earnable Currency - *Future Expansion*):** Earned through weekly challenges, used to bypass cooldowns, refill hearts instantly, or unlock cosmetic avatar frames.

## Retention Loops
1. **Short-Term (Session):** Complete a quest -> Earn XP -> See Fog of War clear slightly -> Anticipate next session.
2. **Mid-Term (Weekly):** Maintain Streak -> Evolve Identity Title -> Complete Weekly Challenge -> Earn minor Artifact.
3. **Long-Term (Monthly/Yearly):** Master an entire World -> Earn a Legendary Artifact -> Cement legacy in the Hall of Fame.
