# 05 - Design Language

## Visual Philosophy: Premium Dark Mode RPG
LearnSomethingDaily is not a classroom; it is a sanctuary. The aesthetic should feel like a high-end video game menu (e.g., Destiny 2, Valorant, or Discord's premium tiers).

### 1. Core Colors
- **Background:** Deep Space Black / Very Dark Green (e.g., `#0f170f`). It must feel vast and infinite.
- **Brand Primary:** Electric Purple/Violet (`#7C4DFF`). Used for primary actions, XP, and high-tier energy.
- **Brand Gold:** Vibrant Gold (`#F7C948`). Reserved *exclusively* for premium achievements, Streaks, and the Artifact Vault.
- **Brand Success:** Neon Green (`#4CAF50`). Used for completed nodes and positive reinforcement.
- **Brand Error:** Warning Red (`#FF6B6B`). Used for failed quests and destructive actions.

### 2. The Artifact Vault (Collectibles)
- **Common (Gray):** Flat design, subtle border.
- **Rare (Blue/Purple):** Glowing border, slight micro-animation on hover.
- **Epic (Gold):** Heavy drop shadow, metallic gradients, pulsing animation.
- *Rule:* Artifacts must feel weighty and valuable. They should use high-quality emojis or 3D renders with distinct background gradients.

### 3. The Knowledge Atlas (The Map)
- **Visible Nodes:** Brightly colored, clear iconography, pulsing rings for the 'active' topic.
- **Hidden Nodes (Fog of War):** Dashed borders, heavy opacity (e.g., 30%), monochromatic or grayscale. Hovering should indicate "Unlock previous region to reveal."
- *Rule:* The map must feel non-linear. Avoid straight vertical lists. Use absolute positioning or organic grids to simulate an actual universe.
